## Approved revision · 30 October 2026
User selected the attached signature palette and approved the testing-led website revision. This website-specific direction supersedes the old dark-only palette for this project only; the reusable design system is not silently rewritten.

Body muted text #625E54; essential input/rule boundaries #80796B. Brass is not small text on ivory. Cormorant Garamond 500–600 headings, Inter body/UI.
